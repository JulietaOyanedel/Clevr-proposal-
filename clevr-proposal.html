03What has changed03 / 10

Prepay, hidden publishers, full price for repeat buyers: all three are gone.

Last time, you prepaid a budget, you could not see which publishers sent your orders, and too many of those orders came from customers you already had.

We rebuilt the model around exactly those three points.

How it works now

You pay weekly, after the orders arrive. We finance your traffic. Every Monday you get an itemised invoice for last week's orders, due Wednesday. Nothing is paid in advance.

You see every publisher, every week. Each week we name the publisher behind every click and order, and the kind of traffic it is.

Full price only for new customers. A higher CPA for new customers and a lower one for returning customers.

  2. PublishersWe choose and place the publishers who fit your customer.
  3. TrackingEvery order is tracked, with refunds and cancellations netted out.
  4. PayoutsWe pay the publishers. You pay us per verified order.

07How you judge us07 / 10

Three numbers decide whether you keep us.

I call them the three kings. If we are winning all three, you scale us. If we are not, you fire us.

  1. What a customer costs you: our CPA against your Meta CAC.
  2. What each order is worth: your average order and return on ad spend, unchanged or better.
  3. How many are new to you: we target more than 60 percent new customers in month one and 70 percent after that.

How we count a sale

An order counts when our platform tracks it. Refunds and cancellations are netted out of your weekly invoice, and fraudulent orders are excluded.

A new customer is a first-time customer of your store, and your existing customer list is suppressed from day one.

08Your numbers08 / 10

Start small: one partner, about 35 orders a week, then build.

Week one

5 orders a day × 7 days = about 35 orders, billed the following Monday.

  • New customer CPAset with your paid team, fixed for the term
  • Returning customer CPAset with your paid team, lower than the new customer rate

The rate is guaranteed. Volume is not, and we will never pretend otherwise.

The aim is not your biggest channel. It is an incremental, profitable 10 to 20 percent of DTC sales in four to six months.
